## What StepRings does

- **Steps and Activity together:** today's steps against your goal, beside Move, Exercise and Stand from Apple Health.
- **Five designs:** Rings, Tracks, Columns, Milestones or Tiles, in rainbow or your own colors.
- **Step Counter:** a widget and Watch complication that counts down the steps left to your goal, or counts up the steps you've taken. You choose for each one:
  - **iPhone:** touch and hold the widget, tap **Edit Widget**, and set **Count** to **Countdown** or **Count up**.
  - **Apple Watch:** pick **Step Counter: Countdown** or **Step Counter: Count up** when you add the complication.
  - On Apple Watch, tap it for a live full-screen counter that counts the same way and moves as you walk.
- **History:** your steps by hour, day, week, month and year, with your goal line, streak and best day.
- **Widgets and complications:** on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, watch face and Smart Stack.
- **Siri:** "How many steps have I taken in StepRings?"
- **Private:** StepRings only reads from Apple Health and keeps everything on your devices. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

## What's new in 1.2.13

- **Step Counter:** the Steps Remaining widget and complication are now the Step Counter. Customize each one to count down (the default) or count up: edit the widget on iPhone, or choose the version when you add the complication on Apple Watch.
- **Rainbow everywhere:** every ring starts in rainbow, one color that follows your progress from red to violet. Empty rings stay grey.
- **A new look:** a new app icon, and colors that are StepRings' own.
- **Simpler Settings:** the design and its colors are together, the section is named after your design, and the Step count and Lanes options are gone.
- **Clearer Apple Health status:** Settings shows **Connected** once StepRings has read your steps.
- **Smaller Watches:** the full-screen counter fits 40 mm and 41 mm screens, also at the largest text size.
- **Requirements:** iOS 18 and watchOS 11 or later.
