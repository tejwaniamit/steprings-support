# StepRings Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 22, 2026

StepRings shows your daily steps, and your Move, Exercise and Stand rings, on your iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## In short

- **We do not collect, store or sell your health data.** StepRings only reads it from Apple Health so it can show it to you.
- Your data never leaves your devices. StepRings has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.
- We never share your data with anyone, because we never receive it.

## The health data StepRings reads

With your permission, StepRings **reads** these from Apple Health:

- your step count (today, and your daily totals for History);
- your Activity summary: Move, Exercise and Stand, with their goals.

StepRings **never writes** to Apple Health and never changes your Health data. On Apple Watch, with your permission, it also reads the Watch's pedometer (Motion & Fitness) while the Steps Remaining screen is open, so the count updates as you walk.

You choose what StepRings may read, and you can change that at any time: on iPhone, in **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → StepRings**; on Apple Watch, in **Settings → Health**.

## What stays on your devices

To show your widgets, complications and History without asking Apple Health every time, StepRings keeps a small copy of recent values on your own iPhone and Apple Watch:

- today's step total and Move, Exercise and Stand progress;
- your daily step totals (up to a year), which your iPhone passes to your Apple Watch for its History screen;
- your settings, such as your goal, colors and design;
- a short log of recent updates, used only to diagnose problems on the device.

This copy lives only on your devices. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Your iPhone and Apple Watch exchange it directly with each other through Apple's Watch Connectivity. Deleting StepRings removes it.

## Siri

If you ask Siri about your steps using a StepRings shortcut, StepRings answers on your device from Apple Health. Apple processes your Siri request under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, store, sell, rent or share your health data or any other personal information.
- We do not use your data for advertising, marketing or profiling.
- We do not track you across apps or websites.
- We do not upload your data to iCloud or to any server.

## Children

StepRings collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If we ever change how StepRings handles your data, we will say so here before the change takes effect.
