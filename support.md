# StepRings Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email [step.rings.dev@gmail.com](mailto:step.rings.dev@gmail.com). Please include your iPhone and Apple Watch models, and the iOS and watchOS versions, if something isn't working.

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 18 or later (iPhone XS, XR, SE 2nd generation or newer)
- For the Watch app: Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later (Series 6, SE 2nd generation, Ultra or newer), paired with that iPhone
- Apple Health access to Steps and Activity

## Common questions

**StepRings shows zero steps.**
StepRings reads your steps from Apple Health. Check that it may read Steps and Activity: on iPhone, open **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → StepRings**; on Apple Watch, open **Settings → Health**. Apple Health doesn't tell apps whether access was denied, so a count of zero can mean no steps yet or no access.

**The Watch or a complication is behind Apple Health.**
Apple Watch saves your steps to Apple Health in batches. Opening StepRings on your iPhone sends its total to your Watch, and the Watch never shows less than that.

**The count on the Steps Remaining screen moves as I walk. Where does that come from?**
While that screen is open, StepRings counts with your Watch's pedometer, so you don't have to wait for Apple Health. It needs Motion & Fitness access, which StepRings asks for the first time you open that screen. Without it, the screen shows Apple Health's count instead.

**How do I change my goal?**
In StepRings on your iPhone, open **Settings** and adjust **Daily goal** (1,000 to 50,000 steps). Your Watch updates on its own.

**Does StepRings collect my data?**
No. StepRings only reads your data from Apple Health and keeps it on your devices. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
