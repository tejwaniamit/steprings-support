# StepRings Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email [step.rings.dev@gmail.com](mailto:step.rings.dev@gmail.com). Please include your iPhone and Apple Watch models, and the iOS and watchOS versions, if something isn't working.

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 18 or later (iPhone XS, XR, SE 2nd generation or newer)
- For the Watch app: Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later (Series 6, SE 2nd generation, Ultra or newer), paired with that iPhone
- Apple Health access to Steps and Activity

## Common questions

### Getting started

**What does StepRings show?**
Today's steps against your daily goal, together with your Move, Exercise and Stand progress from Apple Health. You see it in the iPhone app, the iPhone widgets, the Apple Watch app and the Watch complications.

**How do I connect Apple Health?**
Open StepRings on your iPhone and tap **Connect Apple Health**. Allow StepRings to read Steps and Activity. Open StepRings on your Watch once and allow access there too.

**StepRings shows zero steps.**
StepRings reads your steps from Apple Health. Check that it may read Steps and Activity:
- On iPhone, open **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → StepRings**.
- On Apple Watch, open **Settings → Health**.

Apple Health doesn't tell apps whether access was denied, so a count of zero can mean no steps yet or no access.

**The Move, Exercise or Stand ring is missing or empty.**
These come from your Apple Watch's Activity data in Apple Health. Check that StepRings may read Activity. You set the Move, Exercise and Stand goals in Apple's Fitness app, not in StepRings.

**Does StepRings need an Apple Watch?**
No. The iPhone app and widgets work on their own. They show steps counted by your iPhone and by any other device that saves steps to Apple Health. The Activity rings need an Apple Watch.

### Goal and display

**How do I change my goal?**
In StepRings on your iPhone, open **Settings** and adjust **Daily goal** (1,000 to 50,000 steps). You can also tap **Type a goal…** to enter an exact number. Your Watch updates on its own.

**Can I change the look?**
Yes. In **Settings → Display** you can choose:
- **Progress design:** Rings, Lanes, Tracks, Columns, Milestones or Tiles.
- **Appearance:** System, Light or Dark. This applies to the iPhone app and widgets. Apple Watch always uses a dark display.
- **Step count:** Exact (12,345) or Compact (12.3k).

The **Preview** at the top of Settings shows each change as you make it.

**Can I reorder, hide or recolor the rings?**
Yes. In **Settings**, under **Rings · outer to inner**:
- Tap **Edit** and drag to reorder the rings.
- Use the switches to hide rings. At least one ring stays on, and hidden rings are hidden in the widgets too.
- Use the color menu beside each ring to pick a color, **Rainbow** or **Custom Color…**.

**Do my settings reach my Watch?**
Yes. The goal, progress design, step count style, ring order, visibility and colors sync to your Apple Watch automatically. Keep the Watch near the iPhone, and open StepRings on the Watch if it hasn't updated.

**How do I start over?**
In **Settings**, tap **Reset StepRings Settings**. This restores the 10,000-step goal, the ring order, visibility, colors and display options. Your Health data is not affected.

### History

**Where is my step history?**
On iPhone, tap **History** on the home screen. On Apple Watch, tap the chart button at the top of the Today screen. Choose **Day** (by hour), **Week** or **Month** (by day), or **Year** (by month).

On iPhone, touch a bar to see its steps. The summary shows your goal line, your streak and your best day. The Watch lists every value beside its bar.

**The Watch history is shorter than the iPhone's.**
Your Watch keeps only part of your Health history. StepRings on your iPhone sends your Watch a year of daily totals. Open StepRings on your iPhone once and the Watch shows the full history. Until then, the Watch shows a note.

### Widgets and complications

**What widgets are there?**
On iPhone:
- **StepRings** (Home Screen small, medium and large, and the three Lock Screen sizes) shows your rings with today's values.
- **Steps Remaining** (Home Screen small and medium, and the three Lock Screen sizes) counts down to your goal, then shows how far ahead you are.

On Apple Watch, the **StepRings** widget (rectangular) and the **Steps Remaining** complication (circular, rectangular and inline) do the same on your watch face and in the Smart Stack.

**How do I add a widget or complication?**
- **iPhone:** touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap **Edit** or **Customize**, then add StepRings.
- **Apple Watch:** touch and hold the watch face, tap **Edit**, then choose StepRings for a complication slot.

**A widget isn't up to date.**
iOS and watchOS decide when widgets refresh. StepRings asks for a refresh when your steps go up by 25 or more, or when your goal, the day or a ring changes.

If you force-quit StepRings, iOS doesn't wake it in the background, so widgets wait until you open the app again. Opening StepRings updates its widgets right away.

**The Watch or a complication is behind Apple Health.**
Apple Watch saves your steps to Apple Health in batches. Opening StepRings on your iPhone sends its total to your Watch, and the Watch never shows less than that.

### The countdown on Apple Watch

**How do I open the full-screen countdown?**
Tap the **Steps Remaining** complication on your watch face. It opens a large countdown of the steps left to your goal, or how far ahead you are.

**The count on the countdown moves as I walk. Where does that come from?**
While the countdown is open, StepRings counts with your Watch's pedometer, so you don't have to wait for Apple Health. It needs Motion & Fitness access, which StepRings asks for the first time you open the countdown. Without it, the countdown shows Apple Health's count instead.

The count starts from your Apple Health total and never goes backwards while you watch. The pedometer runs only while the countdown is on screen.

**Why does it say "Updating…"?**
StepRings is reading your latest Apple Health total so the countdown starts from the right number. This usually takes a moment. If Health doesn't answer, the countdown shows the pedometer's count instead.

**Does my Watch tap when I reach my goal?**
Yes. Your Watch plays a short haptic when you reach your goal, once per day.

### Siri

**Can I ask Siri for my steps?**
Yes, on iPhone or Apple Watch, with no setup. Include "StepRings" in the phrase:
- "How many steps have I taken in StepRings?"
- "How many steps are left in StepRings?"
- "Am I ahead of my goal in StepRings?"

Without the app name, Siri answers from Apple Health instead.

### Privacy

**Does StepRings collect my data?**
No. StepRings only reads your data from Apple Health and keeps it on your devices. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it never writes to Apple Health. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

**How do I remove StepRings' data?**
Delete the app. Its saved totals and settings are removed with it. Your Apple Health data stays as it is.
